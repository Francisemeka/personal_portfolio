About

The developer
behind the work.

I'm Onoyima Francis a full stack developer based in Lagos, Nigeria, with 5+ years building the systems that power real products.

My background spans aviation safety platforms, fintech trading systems, healthcare software, Web3 infrastructure and e-commerce across clients in the US, UK, Canada and Scotland. I work best where the problem is complex and the stakes are real.

I'm comfortable across the full stack: PHP, Node.js, Python and Solidity on the backend; React, JavaScript, HTML and CSS on the frontend; MySQL and PostgreSQL for database. I understand authentication, API integration, database architecture and how to build systems that are both secure and maintainable.

I studied Computer Science at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, where I also led a team building the university's website. I've since worked with startups, remote teams and research institutions delivering software that ships and holds up.
